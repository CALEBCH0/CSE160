Controls & Info

Use ◀️ Left / ▶️ Right to switch lanes

Collect coins for +100 points

“Life” gives one immunity

“Boost” doubles speed for 5 seconds

Press X to toggle Debug mode

Use WASD in Debug mode to fly around

Avoid cars (red), barrels, and rocks

(All primary shapes are textured: spheres, cylinders, cubes)

Extra feature: obstacle runner game inspired by Subway Surfers
