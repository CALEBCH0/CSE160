Controls & Information

Use ◀️ Left / ▶️ Right to switch lanes

Collect coins for +100 points each

“Life” gives one immunity to collision

“Boost” doubles your speed for 5 seconds

Press X to toggle Debug/Creative mode

Use WASD to move in the creative mode

Avoid cars (red), barrels, and rock obstacles

Primary shapes(all textured):

- 20 starts and 1 Moon in the sky(spheres)

- 5 barrels(cylinders) and rocks(cubes)

3D models: cars, coins, items, buildings, and road

Feature: obstacle runner game inspired by subway surfer with different items
